THE PINKCITY ADVANTAGE

Engineered For Luxury, Built For Lifetime Durability

Combining European precision extrusion with Japanese 2G hot-melt lamination, Pinkcity Modern PVC Panels brings high-end texture realism, zero VOC emission, and flawless acoustic louvers.

2G Hot-Melt Lamination

Advanced polymer bonding technology ensures zero film peeling, ultra-scratch resistance, and high-temperature thermal stability under extreme conditions.

100% Water & Moisture Proof

Ideal for humid environments, wet area feature walls, kitchens, and coastal regions without swelling, warping, or fungal corrosion.

Zero Termite & Pest Free

100% synthetic high-density virgin PVC core eliminates wood-rot, termite decay, and pest maintenance forever.

B1 Grade Fire Retardant

Self-extinguishing material formulation that resists ignition and flame spread, satisfying international architectural safety codes.

Eco-Friendly & Non-Toxic

Zero formaldehyde, low VOC emissions, 100% recyclable lead-free formulation ensuring healthy indoor air quality for living spaces.

Quick Interlocking System

Seamless tongue-and-groove joint profile enables rapid, dust-free installation directly onto plastered walls, plywood, or metallic frames.
