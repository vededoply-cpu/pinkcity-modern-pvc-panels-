INSTALLATION WORKFLOW

4-Step Tongue & Groove Rapid Fitting

Our interlocking edge design allows dust-free installation directly onto plastered walls, plywood, or aluminum framing.

1

Surface Leveling

Ensure target wall is dry, clean, and level. Apply base plywood grid if wall has severe unevenness.

2

Silicon Adhesive

Apply architectural grade polyurethane silicon sealant or liquid nails onto back ribs of the panel.

3

Interlock & Pin

Slide tongue into groove of previous panel. Secure hidden lip using stainless steel headless pins.

4

Trim & Profile

Finish top and side borders with matching L-trim or metallic end profiles for a polished luxury finish.
